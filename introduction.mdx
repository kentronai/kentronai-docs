Receipt is an AI chat platform on Kentron. You send a message, and Receipt decides how to handle it: some turns get an answer right away, and others become durable background work that keeps running while you do something else.

## How every turn is handled

Every message you send first runs through a classification-only router call. The router does no other work — it only returns one of three decisions:

- **Answer directly.** A second, profile-aware pass writes the response you see in the thread.
- **Author an organization skill.** Receipt drafts or saves a new skill for your organization to reuse.
- **Enqueue a durable background objective.** Receipt queues the work and streams its progress back into the thread as it runs.

When the router's response can't be parsed, Receipt fails closed to the background objective path rather than guessing at a direct answer.

## Every turn leaves a receipt

Receipt records every turn as a hash-chained receipt. Because each receipt links to the one before it, you can replay a run and see exactly what happened at each step, in order.

## Naming: Receipt, Kentron, and Beetle

The product is **Receipt**, and it runs on the **Kentron** platform. The hosted application is at `app.kentron.ai`.

<Note>
The shipped interface labels several areas **Beetle** — Beetle Chat, Beetle Tasks, and the Beetle runners widget. Use it to match this documentation to what's in front of you.
</Note>

## The sidebar you'll see

The sidebar renders, in order: **New**, **Beetle Chat**, **Slack**, **MCP Gateway**, **Model Gateway**, and **Agents**. If you're an owner or admin, you'll also see the administration areas below those. The footer carries a **Docs** link and the **Beetle runners** widget.
