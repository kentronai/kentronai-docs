6. **Read repair without `RECEIPT_INTEGRATIONS_DATABASE_URL`**: what the HTTP-only path can and cannot reconcile, and what happens when Nango's DB is unreachable, is thin (§3.4 one paragraph).
7. **`/connect/connectors` and `/connect/nango/health` response shapes** (fields such as `connectable`, `authMode`, dynamic rows) are not documented beyond a line each.
8. **`expired` status transitions and `expires_at`**: where a row becomes `expired`, and how the UI/tools treat it versus `invalid`, is not covered.
