Everything below happens at `app.kentron.ai`. On the hosted app, sign-up is self-service — there's no invitation required to create your own account.

## Signing up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Go to `/auth/sign-up` and enter your email, a password, and confirm it. Your password must be between 8 and 128 characters. Depending on what's wrong, you'll see `Please enter a valid email address`, `Password must be at least 8 characters`, or `Passwords do not match`.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Verify your email">
    Receipt emails you a six-digit code. It's valid for five minutes and you get five attempts. Run out, and you'll see `You entered too many incorrect codes. Request a new one to continue.`
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receipt sets up your organization">
    As soon as your account exists, Receipt creates an organization named `<Display Name>'s Workspace`, writes its billing baseline, and grants you a one-time platform credit of $5.00 (one-time, per user). That credit is granted once per user, not once per organization — it doesn't repeat if you're later added to a second organization.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Land in chat">
    You're redirected straight to the chat page. There's no onboarding wizard to click through first.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Signing back in

If you forget your password, Receipt emails you a reset code rather than a reset link. Using it also revokes all of your other active sessions.

If your account already has two-factor authentication set up, you'll see a verification step the next time you sign in. There's currently no way to turn two-factor authentication on from within the product — if your account doesn't already have it, you can't enable it yourself today.

## Account settings

Your account page covers:

- **Avatar** — accepts JPG, PNG, WEBP, or SVG, up to 10 MB. A larger file is rejected with `File exceeds limit of 10MB.`
- **Display name** — capped at 32 characters.
- **Interface language** — your preferred language for the interface.

## Security

The security page lists your connected login methods, and won't let you unlink your last one — it's guarded by `You must keep at least one login method connected.` From the same page you can change your password and revoke sessions, either one at a time or all other sessions at once.

<Info>
Sessions last seven days, refreshed on a one-day sliding window each time you use one, and you can be signed in on up to ten devices at once.
</Info>
